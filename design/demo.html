Design system

Every component on one page. Read DESIGN.md for the rules.

Type

Instrument Sans, width 96, weight 450, 14px on 22px, 0.3px tracking. This is the only text setting in the UI.

Nothing is bigger, smaller or bolder. Emphasis comes from the text colour.

Three text colours, all made from one ink. Inverted surfaces (popover, date picker, tooltip, toast, or the inverse class) get matched values: secondary is 40% there instead of 45%.

Buttons

Primary underlines on hover. Secondary goes primary and underlines on hover. Actions sit 1.2em apart. Delete arms on the first click and runs on the second; leaving the row disarms it.

Tabs

One component for mode switches and presets. Options sit 0.35em apart with a / between them and wrap when out of room. The chosen one is primary with no underline and can't be clicked; the rest are secondary.

Names are primary. Hover shows a secondary underline, the selected row a primary one. Hover a row for its action; rows grow in and collapse out (try New item and Delete).

Form

Filled with the label inside. The fill darkens on hover and returns to resting while typing. Disabled inputs use the lightest fill and the disabled colour.

In-place editing

Total INR 40,800. Hover shows a filled container around it. Click the value, type, Enter commits, Escape reverts, Tab moves on.

Icons

Phosphor, two sizes only: 16px regular by default, and 12px, which is always the bold variant.

Dialog and popover

The date picker takes a minimum: days before it are disabled (here, before today, standing in for the issue date).
